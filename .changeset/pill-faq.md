Add `<lw-pill-faq>`: a FAQ as a conversation in a dashed frame with dashed guides down its sides. Questions are pills with a round plus at the end of the row, and an open answer arrives as a reply bubble with the face of the person who answers. Two of the library's buttons close the frame and fire `lw-action`; the primary tokens give the main one a colour of its own.
