Add `<lw-forum-thread>`: a thread on a forum. Its head holds the breadcrumbs, the title with a label such as a version, who started it and when, its replies and views, Watch, and a main action such as Download that keeps its reason readable when it is not allowed, with tabs to the thread's other pages under it. Each post carries its author beside it, with their avatar, whether they are around, title, role and badges, then its time, edit, a button that copies its link and its number; its body takes paragraphs, quotes that fold past a few lines, pictures, code with a copy button and resource links, and its foot Reply and reactions at the lower left (reactions on the posts of the member who started the thread, `react-on="all"` for every post) and its time at the lower right, with its link and number showing on hover. The thread's author and the solution are marked, pages of posts are offered above and below, and the member signed in gets `<lw-post-composer>` at the end (`lw-post`), reached from Reply across from the tabs or at the top right; a post's Reply mentions its author. Guests see no Reply. Beside a post its author keeps in view as the page scrolls (`--lw-forum-thread-sticky-top` clears a navbar); below 44rem each author stands over their post. `headless` leaves the thread's head out, keeping its title, who started it and when in a row across from Reply.
