Add `<lw-user-hover-card>`: a member's card over their name in a forum. Resting the pointer on the name opens it over the line pointed at, or under it with no room above, kept inside the window and following the name as the page scrolls: their banner with the avatar on its lower edge, name, role and whether they are around, bio, posts, reactions and accepted solutions, when they joined and where they are, and Follow and Message. The pointer can cross to the card, moving on to another name opens its card at once, keyboard focus opens it with Tab going on into it and Escape closing it, and on a touch screen the first tap opens it and the next follows the link.
