Add `<lw-trending-media>`: a forum widget of the media drawing the most attention. Each row holds its thumbnail, marked when it is a video, audio or an embed, its title over two lines at most, the album or category it is in, who added it and when, with the member's card over their name, and its comments. The whole row leads to the media, the album and the name to theirs, and the times count on as the page stays open; a thumbnail that fails to load shows a placeholder.
