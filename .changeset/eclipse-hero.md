Add `<lw-eclipse-hero>`: a hero framed by a total eclipse drawn in CSS. The copy sits on the moon while the corona glows around it, its streamers turning slowly under fine engraved rays. On the first view the moon slides into place until a sliver of sun and a bright bead show at the upper right, the diamond ring, and the light then follows the pointer round the rim. It follows the theme: white light in a starry sky on a dark page, ink on paper on a light one. On narrow screens the eclipse moves above the copy. The announcement pill links when given `announcement-href`, and a short `note` sits under the two actions. The corona takes a colour through `--lw-eclipse-hero-glow`. The motion rests off screen and stays still with reduced motion.
