The gooey dock, dropdown, navigation menu, search and toolbar now pour their shapes in a surface of the page's own scheme, white in a light theme and a step lighter than the page in a dark one, with the text colour on them, instead of turning the theme over. A hairline edge and a soft shadow lift the shapes off the page. `fill` and `ink`, and their CSS properties, still set colours of your own. The gooey slider keeps the text colour for its value, like `<lw-range>`.
