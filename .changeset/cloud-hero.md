Add `<lw-cloud-hero>`: a centred hero under a sky of slowly drifting clouds drawn by a small WebGL shader, banks rising from the bottom corners and wisps crossing the top while the band behind the copy stays clear, lit from the upper right. The sky is a CSS gradient from the first paint and the fallback without WebGL; it follows the theme, takes a palette through custom properties, draws at a reduced size and rate, rests off screen and while the page scrolls, holds still for reduced motion and pauses from its corner button.
