Add `<lw-member-profile>`: a member's page on a forum. Its head carries their banner, a large avatar with whether they are around, their name, role and title, what they are doing now, when they joined, where they are and their website, a short bio, and their messages, reaction score, solutions and points; a visitor finds Follow, Message and Report, and the member on their own page Edit profile and Edit banner. Tabs under it hold the profile posts left for them, with a field to write one, their latest activity down a timeline, the threads they started, and about them in full, the arrow keys moving between the tabs. Every name opens the member's card, and the head lays itself out for the room it has.
