Add `<lw-member-profile>`: a member's page on a forum. Its head carries their banner, a large avatar with whether they are around and, beside it in a thought bubble, their status (`customStatus`); under the avatar their name, role and title, what they are doing now, when they joined, where they are and their website, a short bio, and their messages, reaction score, solutions and points. A visitor finds Follow and Message across from the avatar and Report on the banner. On their own page the member finds Edit profile across from the avatar, its pencil alone on a phone; resting the pointer on the banner or the avatar shows a pencil of dark glass over it and the whole of either edits it, and the status bubble edits the status or offers to set one (`lw-edit` with `part` profile, banner, avatar or status). Tabs under the head hold the profile posts left for them, with a field to write one, their latest activity down a timeline, the threads they started, and about them in full, the arrow keys moving between the tabs. Every name opens the member's card, and the head lays itself out for the room it has.
