Add `<lw-notable-members>`: a forum's notable members. Beside a menu of its lists, an overview shows the first few on each, such as the most messages, the highest reaction score, the most solutions and points, today's birthdays and the staff, each card with See all to its full list, ranked with the first three marked and each member's title. Find member looks through everyone on the lists as you type and offers the matches as links, Enter handing the search to the page (`lw-find`); `view` and `lw-view` let the page follow what shows. Every name opens the member's card, and narrower than 52rem the menu runs along the top.
