Add `<lw-forum-sidebar>`: a forum's sidebar. Its pages come first, Home, Topics, Members and the rest, with More folding open for the others, then its categories, each a square in the colour the forum search gives it, and its tags, each list under a heading that folds it away. The page you are on keeps a background of its own while a lighter pill slides between the links under the pointer, and the arrow keys move through the links. Until the page scrolls the sidebar is bare beside it; once it scrolls it takes the navbar's double frame of frosted glass, clear of the window's edges, and stays the window's height, its links scrolling inside and fading out at an edge with more past it. At its foot the forum software's `version` and the copyright (`company`) face a button that closes it: the sidebar slides out to the left, the page takes its column and a button left in the lower left corner brings it back, the focus moving to whichever button undoes it, and the buttons darken a little when pressed or hovered, or lighten in a dark theme; `closed` sets it and `lw-toggle` tells of it. Below 64rem it is a button at the top naming the page you are on, dark on a light theme and light on a dark one, that spreads across the window as its links fold open under it. With `navbar` it stands under `<lw-forum-navbar sidebar>`: at the top the bar spans the window and the sidebar sits under it at the left, and as the page scrolls the bar shrinks from the sidebar to the right edge while the sidebar rises into the room it leaves, the two taking their frames together; closed, the sidebar gives the bar that room back. Below 64rem it then opens as a drawer from the navbar's button instead: a modal dialog sliding in from the window's left edge over the dimming page, the focus on the page you are on, and out again on Escape, a press on the page, a link or the button at its foot, the page never scrolling under it.
