Add `<lw-forum-navbar>`: the floating navbar for a forum. At the top of the page it spans the full width; once the page scrolls it shrinks into the floating frame, the sign-in button down to its account icon and, while the links show, the brand to its mark, without moving the page under it. The site's menus sit beside the brand and the forum's sections, Latest, Media and Members, face them from the far side. A search button beside the theme switch, or ⌘K / Ctrl+K, shrinks the bar to a search field across all of it and grows it down to hold a panel: before anything is typed it shows how to search, words for titles, `@member` and `#category` to filter, typing `@` or `#` lists the members or categories to finish with, and the threads found list under their filters, each removable, all from the keyboard too. Signed in, `user` puts the member's `<lw-profile-dropdown>` where Sign In was, an outlined button with their avatar and name, their role across from the avatar at the menu's head, Profile and Settings in it, the avatar alone once the bar shrinks; the menu sets the theme, so the theme switch steps aside. On a narrow screen the links fold into the menu as groups of their own while search and Sign In stay on the bar as icons.
