Add `<lw-profile-posts>`: a forum widget of the latest posts members left on profiles, each with its author, the member it was left for, its text folding under Show more past four lines, any picture, and when. With `user` a status field at the top posts with its Post button or ⌘/Ctrl and Enter: `lw-post` fires first, and unless it is cancelled the post goes to the top at once. Without a `heading` it leaves its head out, as on a member's page, and `empty` says what shows while there are no posts.
