Add `<lw-support-card-faq>`: numbered questions beside a support card that stays in view on wide screens. The card shows the faces of the team that replies ending in a place for the reader, a title, a sentence and a button; the open question turns over in the opposite scheme and its plus becomes a minus. The card is turned over by default, or takes `--lw-support-card-faq-accent` and `--lw-support-card-faq-on-accent`.
