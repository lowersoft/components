Add `floating` to `<lw-text-input>`, `<lw-password-input>`, `<lw-number-input>` and `<lw-textarea>`: the label sits inside the box over the empty control and rises, smaller, once the field is focused, typed in, set or autofilled, lined up with the value after any icon or button. The placeholder waits until the label is out of its way, text at the ends moves onto the value's line, a textarea scrolls its text under a strip rather than the raised label, and `floating="always"` keeps the label up with the placeholder showing.
