Add `<lw-metric-testimonials>`: named customer proof in a bento grid, led by a number. Over it sit an eyebrow framed in corner marks and a headline in two tones. A tall card opens on the headline metric, whose digits roll up the first time it comes into view; a wide card leads with a metric of its own, and two compact cards sit under it, one turned over in the opposite scheme. Each quote opens on a pair of quote marks and closes on the person, with the company's mark at the end of the row, and a rating row links to every review. The quote marks and corners take a colour through `--lw-metric-testimonials-accent`. This adds `QUOTE_ICON` to `core/testimonial.js`.
