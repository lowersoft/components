Add `<lw-four-column-footer>`: three bands divided by hairlines that run edge to edge, the brand with a description, the email address and social links; four columns of links beside a stamp and the copyright; and the brand's name in heavy lowercase with a square full stop, sized to the band and standing on the footer's foot. Lowersoft's logo colours carry the scheme: the name fades into the badge's navy, its full stop is the wordmark's red square, links under the mouse and the stamp are marked with its orange one, and an orange light follows the mouse across the name. Each colour can be changed with `--lw-four-column-footer-accent`, `--lw-four-column-footer-dot` and `--lw-four-column-footer-word`.

The theme gains Lowersoft's logo colours as tokens, the same in either scheme: `--lw-brand-orange`, `--lw-brand-red` and `--lw-brand-navy`.
