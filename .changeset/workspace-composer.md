Add `<lw-workspace-composer>`: an assistant feature beside its composer. The copy column holds an eyebrow, the headline, a sentence, points and two actions; the composer card floats over a softened landscape, drawn in CSS or from `photo`, with one long request, a model pill carrying Lowersoft's badge and the tools it can reach as chips. Its keys sit in soft inset wells and take no input. The first time it comes into view the request types itself in over its own room, so nothing moves, and the send key lights up once it is written. Three numbered claims run along the bottom, parted by rules. The actions fire `lw-action`, or your own links go in the `actions` slot. This adds the Lucide mic icon to `core/lucide.js` and moves `splitWords` into `core/text.js`.
