`<lw-floating-navbar>` is bare until the page scrolls: no frame, border or background around its links and actions at the top of the page, which it takes on once the page scrolls. Opening the narrow menu gives it that scrolled shape too, until the menu has folded away, so the menu looks the same at the top of the page as further down, on a solid sheet the page never shows through.
