Add `<lw-media-gallery>`: a forum's media as square thumbnails in a grid, two across on a phone up to six on a wide page, with pages of them above and below (`page`, `pages`, `page-href`, `lw-page`). Resting the pointer on a thumbnail, or focusing it, brings up its title, who added it and when, and its reactions and comments over a shade at its foot; on a touch screen they always show. Videos and audio carry their length, embeds a globe, media the member signed in has not seen a New mark, and a thumbnail that fails to load a placeholder. A thumbnail opens the viewer over the darkened page (`lw-open`, cancelable): the image at full size, its thumbnail softened until it arrives, a video in `<lw-video-player>`, audio in `<lw-audio-player-card>` or a link to the site an embed plays on, with its title, who added it, when, its album or category, its counts and a link to its page; the arrow keys, Home and End, the buttons at its sides or a swipe step through, Escape, a press beside it or its close button close it, and the focus goes back to the thumbnail of what it showed last. Opening an item marks it seen (`lw-view`). Filters fold open under the grid's head: a type, newest or oldest first or the most reactions, comments or views, and one member's media, each in use standing across the head as a removable chip; they apply to the items given unless `lw-filter` is canceled. Signed in (`user`), Mark viewed clears the New marks (`lw-mark-viewed`) and Add media asks for more (`lw-add`).
