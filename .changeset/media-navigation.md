Add `<lw-media-navigation>`: the way around a forum's media, in the frame the forum's widgets use. Its categories come first, each with a square in its own colour, or an icon, and how much media it holds, then links to the albums and the member's own media and albums. The page you are on keeps a background, as does the category or album it sits under (`current`, the address bar's by default).
