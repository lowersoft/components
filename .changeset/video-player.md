Add `<lw-video-player>`: a video player in the spirit of Vidstack. Controls fade while the video plays. The timeline is split into chapters, shows what has buffered and previews the frame, chapter and time under the pointer from a thumbnails file. Settings slide between pages for speed, quality, captions, caption style, audio track and looping, and chapters have a menu of their own. The player draws its own captions from WebVTT or SRT, so they clear the controls and take the viewer's style. It plays HLS through hls.js loaded on demand, and files of several heights become a quality menu. It also offers picture-in-picture, fullscreen that turns a phone sideways, AirPlay and casting, and the system's media controls. Below 576 px it switches to a compact touch layout where a double tap seeks. Keyboard shortcuts follow YouTube's and can be remapped. Loading can wait until the browser is idle, the player is on screen or the first play, and `storage` remembers the viewer's choices. Script it like the media element, with `play()`, `seek()`, `currentTime`, `volume` and the rest, plus `subscribe()` to follow a `state` snapshot; the same state is mirrored on `data-*` attributes for styling. This also adds Lucide icons for media controls to `core/lucide.js`.
