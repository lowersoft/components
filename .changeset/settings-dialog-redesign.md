`<lw-settings-dialog>` is redesigned: sections grouped down the side with a sliding highlight, each a titled panel of grouped rows built from the library: a portrait drawn from your name, `<lw-theme-toggle-button>` previewing the theme live, a segmented density choice, signed-in devices and `<lw-confirm-delete-button>` to delete the account (`lw-delete-account`). It scrolls on short screens, and the save bar takes no room until there is something to save.
